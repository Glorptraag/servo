## Findings

- **F1 · minor · rule 7 / D40 · `src/card-game/view.tsx:175` and `:197`.** Stop (on every card) and Back to the parent view (on the closing line) are buttons the child can reach, and both give the whole page back at once, with "n of 10 parts named" for the previous round (Stop) or the round just played (Back). The gate holds the screen only until the adult presses one of them. This is the builder's stated design ("a deliberate step") and the README says so, but a child who taps Stop mid-round sees the earlier score. Default: accept for launch; the gap from the 140 px to one tap is still a real improvement. Cheaper hardening if Drew wants it: Stop and Back return to the idle card-game screen only, with the parent sections released by a separate adult-only control (for example Show the progress, disclosure).
- **F2 · minor · latent · `src/card-game/view.tsx:97-100`.** `onActive(false)` is sent only when `active` changes, never on unmount. If the section ever unmounts mid-round without the view unmounting (a profile removal or `key` change), `inRound` stays true and the page stays blank except for the heading. Not reachable today (Children and the switch are hidden during a round, and the gate closing unmounts the whole view). Default: accept; add an unmount cleanup that calls `onActive(false)` when convenient.
- **N1 · note · `src/accounts/view.tsx:313`.** The page-level `role="status"` line stays on screen during a round. It carries the account actions' messages, not the result, and nothing sets it during a round. Fine; stated for completeness.
