## Findings

1. major · rules 11 and 14 · packages/content/test/arenas.test.ts:5-10 · The hand-written global `ImportMeta.glob` method collides with the `glob` property in `vite/client`. Task 0.4 brings `vite/client` into the same tsc program: `packages/content/src/index.ts` on task/0.4 (19ea280) starts with `/// <reference types="vite/client" />`, and content's tsconfig includes both `src` and `test`. The scratch merge of task/2.4 into task/0.4 has no textual conflict, but `pnpm --filter @servo/content typecheck` fails with `test/arenas.test.ts(8,5): error TS2300: Duplicate identifier 'glob'`. So `pnpm check` and CI go red whichever of the two lands second. The builder followed the schema's precedent (`packages/schema/test/vite-env.d.ts`), but this file is where the fix belongs. Fix: make `/// <reference types="vite/client" />` the first line, delete lines 5-10, and call `import.meta.glob<string>(…)`. I verified the fix on this branch (tsc clean) and in the scratch merge (typecheck, 30 tests and repo lint clean).
2. minor · rule 13 · packages/content/arenas/ramp.json:7-34 · The orchestrator gave the ramp three jobs: gearbox, slip and tipping. Nothing can tip on it, and the builder neither met that nor flagged it.
   - **Tipping.** The reference robots' centre of mass sits 3–9 mm ahead of the chassis centre and about 27 mm above the floor. They tip only on slopes of about 48° or more, but the steepest slope the chassis clears is about 22° (1-in-2.4). Even with the 2-cell pack on the bumper mount, the descent would need to be about 1-in-2.5 to tip it, and this one is 1-in-5. The full width (test:239-246, "so the robot cannot drop off a side") also removes any drop-off.
   - **Gearbox.** Plausible. My rough figures from the example records agree with test:205-207:
     - the Level 1 direct-drive robot needs about 86% of its motors' stall torque;
     - a Level 2 build through the motor driver stalls;
     - with gearboxes there is about 2× margin.
   - **Slip.** Steady climbing does not slip: grip is about 0.45 W against 0.32 W needed. Only rear-heavy builds or start-up would slip.

   See question 1.
