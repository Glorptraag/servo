The check is `startsWith(event + ':')` on the item and `toContain(words)` on the rest. It is a substring match, case-sensitive, over the whole item, not per field. So a one-word phrase such as "which" passes, and "which challenge" in `hint` is satisfied by the same words sitting under `run`.
