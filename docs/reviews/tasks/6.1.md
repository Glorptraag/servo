## Findings

- **F1 · Medium · brief Section 10 (slow motion).** `packages/app/src/spec-card/frames.ts:40`, `packages/app/src/App.tsx:63`. `readoutFrameDue` hands the card a frame only every third tick. At 1 to 5 ticks a second, where `SLOW_MOTION_RATE` says "each tick is a step the child can see", a card readout (a motor's speed, an LED's level) changes only every 3 s at 1 tps and every 0.6 s at 5 tps, while the canvas moves every tick. Numbers shown are still correct for their tick, just late. The throttle exists to save work at 30 tps; slow motion needs no saving. Fix: pass every frame when the clock rate is at or below the slow-motion rate (the loop's state has the rate), or throttle by elapsed time rather than by tick. Add a test at rate 1.
- **F2 · Low · end of Run.** `App.tsx:63`. The last frame of a Run (a finished challenge, a Run that ends on a fault) is pushed only when its tick is a multiple of 3 or a switch or fault changed, so the card can end up to two ticks (66 ms) behind the frame the canvas holds. Non-discrete values (LED lit, motor speed) are not in the change test. Small, but push the loop's final frame regardless.
- **F3 · Low · offline.** `packages/app/src/run-bar/run-loop.ts:14`, `:207`. First Run now needs the lazy chunk. If it fails to load (offline before install finished, or a stale tab after a new release), the error path is the generic one-line failure. Check the line reads sensibly for a child, and consider a retry on the next press (a rejected dynamic import is cached by the module map, so a reload may be needed).
- **F4 · Low · tests.** `packages/app/test/perf/perf.test.ts` guards against static sim-core imports, which is the regression that mattered. Nothing guards `useFrame`'s key (`spec-card.tsx:30`): a stale card when only a non-`closed` value changes is covered by the throttle test only for switches and faults.
- **F5 · Low · docs/perf.md.** The "Before and after" table's Run "before" figures (22.6, 31.6 ms p95) and Round 2's (16.7, 17.6) are different baselines at different load, and the headline iPad "met" rests on Round 2 at load 17 to 20; the same code measured 18.6 to 23.4 ms at load 25 to 40. The text says so, but the Verdict-level summary at the top of the page should say "iPad stand-in met at moderate load, over at heavy load". The "Round 2 ... orchestrator ruling" heading names a ruling a reader of the repo cannot see; point at the decision id.
- **F6 · Low · wording.** perf.md says the 2× slowdown "may flatter or wrong the A12"; read as "or understate".
- **F7 · Nit.** `pnpm perf:app` is deliberately outside `pnpm perf` and CI, so the cold-start budget is guarded only by the bundle test. A regression that moves a heavy import into the start chunk is caught; a slow start from other causes is not.
