## Findings

1. **Medium, `packages/canvas/src/routing/exposure.ts:59`.** `bend` consults sockets and lines above only, never part bodies. A tidied route that was built to keep off pictures (D80/D85) can get a bend that crosses one. The "zero crossings after tidying" assertion (`test/browser/routing.test.ts:~288`) was switched to `routing.tidied`, so it no longer checks the lines as drawn, and nothing asserts the bent routes. Not measured here. Default: add a body-avoiding preference to the bend search (try clear-of-bodies offsets first), and assert crossings on `routes` after a tidy.
2. **Low, `exposure.ts:65-75`.** Offsets move in 1 mm steps with ties broken by fraction and then left before right, so a small move of a part can flip the bend to the other side when both are clear at the same step. A bend also appears or disappears as unrelated lines change, because it is recomputed from scratch. No test covers a small-move flip. Default: accept now; add a hysteresis or a prefer-previous-side rule only if testers see wires jump.
3. **Low, `placement/controller.ts:396`, `:674`.** A loose part tapped off-centre on its own tile while Move is active shifts so its origin lands on the tap (the builder followed the spec). A child who taps the part to inspect it, after Move, nudges it a few millimetres. Default: keep it, since Undo is one tap; revisit if testing shows nudging.
4. **Low, `exposure.ts:19`.** A line is checked in 0.5 mm steps over every socket and every higher line on each rebuild; fine on the busy workbench (32 wires) in the suite. No `pnpm perf` run was done for this review.
5. **Info.** `docs/routing.md` is edited though the task did not list it, which is right: the routing contract changed. The README and `placement.md` decision 7 are consistent with the code.
