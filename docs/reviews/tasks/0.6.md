## Findings

Paths are relative to `packages/tools/` unless stated.

1. minor · rule 12 · src/placeholder-art/forms.ts:146-151 · The DC motor's drawn shaft is not where its record puts it.
   - `motor-can` puts the bearing and shaft hole at the camera end of the longest axis.
   - The fixture DC motor's drive-out comes out of its side: at (10, 13, 10) with axis +y (`packages/schema/fixtures/parts/dc-motor.json:135-143`).
   - So the canvas's shaft socket, projected through `VIEW`, lands on top of the can, about 10 mm (about 30 px of 160) from the drawn shaft.

   - The near strip hides the chip completely, so the tile reads as a green tray with two white walls.
   - Shorter strips would let the chip show.
3. minor · task spec · src/placeholder-art/cli.ts:107, src/placeholder-art/README.md:115 · The registry resolves `identity.art` only.
   - A `card.realWorldArt` key is never resolved, though the schema calls it a swap-registry key.

   - No fixture has one, so nothing fails today. It waits on D32.

   - The orchestrator should settle which contract stands when 0.4 merges.

   - When the two differ, as in the tests, messages print absolute paths.

6. minor · rule 14 · packages/tools/ · The tools package still has no README at its root. The new docs are in `src/placeholder-art/README.md`.

## Questions for Drew

1. D32, the builder's three questions:
   - **Real-world photos.** Should the registry track `card.realWorldArt` keys with no placeholder, so the spec card shows no photo until one is dropped in? Default (D32): yes, as a small tools follow-up that adds them to `registry.json`.
   - **Final formats.** The registry now takes avif, jpeg, jpg, png, svg and webp. D32 recommends SVG, PNG and WebP. Narrowing the list is a one-line change to `FINAL_EXTENSIONS`, and format and background stay open under D6. Default: keep the wider list until D6 is decided.
   - **Rotations.** D32's default is one tile per part, rotated by the canvas. Rotating a three-quarter tile has three side effects:
     - Its light and its "up" turn with it.
     - Three-quarter tiles placed at canvas-plane positions sit off the chassis's drawn mount points, unless the canvas projects positions through the same view.
     - Mirrored parts raise the same issue. `packages/schema/docs/geometry.md` tells the canvas to flip a mirrored part across its own x axis. The 0.6 README (line 110) says the unflipped tile already is the mirror image, and that flipping it puts the light on the wrong side.

     Should Build mode draw parts with these three-quarter tiles, projecting positions through the same view, with four quarter-turn tiles per part? The generator can make those from the same records. Or should the canvas use a top-down view, and keep the three-quarter tiles for the tray, library and spec card? Default: decide before task 3.1 (canvas renderer) starts, and keep one tile until then.
2. The tile and the record disagree on where the DC motor's shaft is. Which should change?
   - (a) Tiles place shafts and hubs from the record's drive ports. That is still data, so ground rule 1 holds.
   - (b) The DC motor record puts its shaft at the end of the can, as on a real motor. This changes the chassis drive layout.

   Default: (a), as a small tools follow-up once task 2.1 authors the real DC motor.
