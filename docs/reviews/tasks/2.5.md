## Questions for Drew

1. **Praise (finding 2).** Should the list also ban these?
   - the interjections and set phrases: hooray, yay, nice one, nicely done, top job, way to go, you got it and spot on;
   - excellent, fantastic, superb, brilliant and perfect.

   Recommendation: ban all of them, and reword any descriptive use, such as "a brilliant white LED". Leave `great` alone off the list, because `great job` and `great work` already cover its praise use.
2. **Rewards beyond the sources.** `stars`, `trophy`, `prize`, `reward` and `XP` pass, as in "Get three stars for this build". Ban them too? Recommendation: yes.
   - None has a Level 1–2 use, and brief Section 8 principle 6 rules out reward tokens.
   - Keep `life`, for "battery life".
   - Keep `unlock`, which the brief uses for levels and settings.
3. **Brief prose against the gloss rule.** These use a gloss alone:
   - the family name "Brain" (brief Section 3; schema `taxonomy.ts:8`);
   - "The brain runs a program" (Section 3);
   - the Rolling Start list "…caster, frame" (Section 4).

   None is content today. But the library shows family labels, and "Brain" would be refused if the UI applies the lists to them. Recommendation: treat family labels as names, not system text, and keep the rule for sentences. The alternative is to rename the family.
4. **Character names in other text.** Only a part's name is checked against the components list, so "Sparky turns electricity into spinning." passes. Recommendation: leave this to review. A capitalised-word check on text would also refuse LED, AA and the first word of every sentence.
5. **Emoji and rare exclamation marks in system text.** The list format cannot hold a symbol, so both rest on the schema.
   - "Turns electricity into spinning 🤖" and "Cross the arena 🎉" pass every check.
   - `‽` (U+203D) and `﹗` (U+FE57) pass `text.exclamation` (packages/schema/src/validate/reader.ts:194).

   Recommendation: refuse pictographs and these marks beside `text.exclamation`, if the schema freeze allows a stricter text check.
6. **Readings to queue under rule 13** (README.md:211-229):
   - a part's name is built from listed terms only, and may carry its own gloss, as in `chassis (frame)`;
   - wire and port terms are real names;
