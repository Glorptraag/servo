## Verification of the asked points

- **Every sound has a visual twin.** I traced each one:
  - motor, hum, buzz, squeal and knock: the canvas draws a twin for each `RunSound` from the same live levels (canvas/src/run-animation/overlay.ts:222-231, 294-375; state.ts:153; PULSE_HZ in look.ts:38).
  - whir: the spin-up, where tick 0 is held and the wires light with flowing dots (canvas/src/run-animation/animator.ts:4). Run also turns to Stop. With reduced motion the dots do not flow, but the wires are drawn lit and the toggle still changes.
  - tick: the Run bar's beat dot, keyed per tick (run-bar.tsx:174, :212-213), under the same `isSlowMotion` rule as the sound.
  - click: the wire's elastic settle (canvas/src/wiring/motion.ts).
- **Mute is reachable by every path.** The control is a native `<button role="switch" aria-checked>` named "Sound" (control.tsx:34). The browser test drives it by pointer, by a real CDP touch, and by Enter. It also checks that Space on the focused switch is still Run (D42).
- **The AudioContext starts only after a gesture.**
  - `WebAudioSink.open()` returns nothing until `unlock()` has run, and nothing while sound is off (synth.ts:136-152).
  - The layer calls `unlock` from capture-phase pointerdown, pointerup, touchend, keydown and click listeners (layer.ts:41, 102-108).
  - Tested in Node (synth.test.ts, "makes no audio before the first gesture" and "never makes a context while sound is off") and in Chromium, with a real AudioContext that is running after the click and suspended with sound off (browser/sound.test.tsx:158-175).
- **Build mode makes no sound except the landing click.**
  - The run-loop cues fire only on loop frames, and the whir only on entering spin-up (layer.ts:118-128).
  - The canvas `edit` event never fires in Run mode (canvas/src/interface.ts:203) and fires only when the build changed. A refused wrong-colour connect does not click.
  - `hush` is silence, not a sound.
  - Tested at layer.test.ts:244-255 and :258-272.
- **Rule 7.** No success, goal or fanfare cue exists. The `SoundCue` union is machine, whir, tick, click and hush (cues.ts:17-30). The control's words are "Sound", "On" and "Off". The one console line has no exclamation mark. All sounds are synthesised one-shots or level-following voices, with no loop and no music.
- **Rules 1–6.** The motor's sound follows the event's level. The buzzer's pitch is the part record's `hz`, carried in the event (synth.ts:44). No part identity is coded into the app. All imports follow the package map, and lint is clean.

## Findings

1. **Low · rule 13 · packages/app/README.md:89, :92.** The builder's two open questions, whether a part landing on a mount should click and whether a shared replay should sound, are written only in the README. They are not in the decision queue: `pharao.py decision list` has no sound entry. The orchestrator should queue them, as Q1 and Q2 below.
2. **Low · robustness · packages/app/src/sound/synth.ts (no visibility handling), packages/app/src/run-bar/run-loop.ts:18.** In a hidden tab, `requestAnimationFrame` stops, so the Run freezes. The held voices keep sounding at their last level, and the AudioContext is not suspended. A child who switches away mid-Run hears a motor or buzzer drone until they come back. The visual twin is frozen at the same time, so this is an annoyance rather than a parity break. Suggested fix: suspend the context, or hush, on `visibilitychange` to hidden, and resume on visible.
3. **Low · robustness · packages/app/src/App.tsx:37-38.** The layer is made in `useState`, and an effect cleanup calls `sound.dispose()`, which closes the sink for good (synth.ts:126-133, `closed = true`). The app does not use StrictMode today, so this is safe. But StrictMode, or anything else that replays effects, would leave the page silent for the rest of the visit. The parent package already uses StrictMode (parent/src/accounts/mount.tsx:17). Suggested fix: make the layer and sink inside the effect, or let `dispose` be undone.
4. **Info · scope · packages/app/src/App.tsx, packages/app/README.md, packages/app/test/.** The task's file scope is `packages/app/src/sound/`. The branch also wires the layer into App.tsx (14 lines, the same pattern as the spec card's `runFrames`), updates the README and adds tests. All three are needed for the done-when and the definition of done. No objection.
5. **Info · tests · packages/app/test/sound/layer.test.ts:263, :265.** The `place-part` and `remove-part` commands are cast `as never`, so their shapes are not type-checked. If `EditCommand` changes, the "no click for other edits" case could quietly test nothing. Use the real command shapes.
6. **Info · twin parity · packages/app/src/run-bar/run-bar.tsx:174 against packages/app/src/sound/layer.ts:126.** In slow motion, the beat dot shows and pulses once for tick 0 during the spin-up, while the tick sound starts at tick 1. Tick 0 is not a step and the whir covers the spin-up, so this is acceptable. It is noted only for completeness.
7. **Info · keyboard · packages/app/src/sound/control.tsx:34.** A `role="switch"` usually toggles with Space, but here Space is Run (D42), so keyboard users toggle sound with Enter. This is consistent with D42 and with the canvas list view. A screen reader's own activation works.
8. **Info · flake, not this branch · packages/app/test/run-bar/record.test.ts:157.** A fixed `setTimeout(resolve, 20)` races the recorder under load. It failed once in a full unit run and passed on four re-runs. It belongs to the test-hardening owner, not to 4.10.

## Judgement on the builder's decisions

- **Sound starts on.** Agreed. The brief gives no default, every sound has a twin, and the setting persists.
- **Squeal is included.** Agreed. Sim-core emits it, the canvas twins it, and it is machine feedback. Leaving it out would break the one-to-one mapping.
- **No click when a part snaps onto a mount.** Defensible, and the conservative reading. Section 11's sound list, which is the brief's sound specification, names only "a click when a wire lands", and so does Section 6, principle 6. Section 10's "parts land with a click" sits in "Motion and timing", next to "wires draw with a short elastic settle". It is ambiguous whether it means a sound or the snap motion. Rule 13 makes it a question for Drew (Q1). Adding the click later is small: listen for `place-part` and `move-part` edits that land on a mount.
- **No workshop ambience.** Agreed. The brief calls it optional, and "no music loop by default" holds.
- **A shared build's replay plays no sound.** Acceptable for now. The replay is watched by an adult on another device, and the sharing page has no sound control to turn it off, so silence is the conservative choice (Q2).

## Questions for Drew

- **Q1.** Should a part landing (dropped onto a mount point or a free spot) also make a click, reading Section 10's "parts land with a click" as a sound? **Default:** no. Only a wire landing clicks, as Section 11's sound list says. If yes, it is a small follow-up in `src/sound/cues.ts`.
- **Q2.** Should a shared build's replay (task 5.6) play the Run's sounds, with its own sound switch? **Default:** no, the replay stays silent, as built.
- **Q3.** Should sound stop while the tab is hidden (finding 2)? **Default:** yes. Hush or suspend when the tab is hidden, and bring the held sounds back when it is shown again, as a small follow-up.
- **Q4.** Should there be a volume control as well as on and off? **Default:** no, only on and off, as built (README decision 7).
